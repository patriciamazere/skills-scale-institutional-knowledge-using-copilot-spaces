# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers translate user needs and business goals into clear, usable product experiences. They design flows, interactions, and interface elements that align with customer expectations and product strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or contribute to user research and usability exploration
- Define information architecture, user flows, and interface patterns
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and visual design specifications
- Partner with Product Managers to validate problem framing and user value
- Work with Developers and QA to confirm implementation matches design intent

### Goals
- Improve usability, clarity, and customer satisfaction
- Ensure the product experience is consistent and accessible
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or inconsistent interfaces

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery and review sessions
- Design critiques with Developers and PMs
- UX validation and acceptance feedback with QA

### Interaction with Other Roles
- With Product Managers: clarify user pain points, success criteria, and design trade-offs
- With Developers: ensure feasible implementation, consistency, and handoff clarity
- With QA: verify accessibility, usability, and acceptance against intended experience
- With Project Managers: surface design dependencies, timeline impacts, and review needs

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts connect business context, stakeholder needs, and delivery execution. They improve requirement clarity and ensure work is framed in a way the team can implement and validate.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify goals, workflows, and business rules from stakeholder input
- Translate business requirements into user stories, backlog items, and acceptance criteria
- Work with Product Managers and stakeholders to resolve ambiguity and scope questions
- Support impact analysis for cross-team dependencies and process changes
- Partner with QA to validate that requirements are testable and complete

### Goals
- Reduce requirement ambiguity and rework
- Improve traceability from business need to delivered outcome
- Strengthen decision quality across planning and execution

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder conversations and requirement workshops
- Backlog refinement and acceptance criteria reviews
- Cross-functional dependency and readiness discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- With Product Managers: refine problem statements, success metrics, and prioritization logic
- With Developers: clarify workflows, edge cases, and implementation assumptions
- With Project Managers: identify schedule, dependency, and readiness risks
- With QA: ensure acceptance criteria are measurable and testable

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, design integrity, and solution quality. They make sure implementation choices support reliability, maintainability, and long-term roadmap alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, architecture patterns, and integration approaches
- Review designs and implementation trade-offs with Developers
- Identify technical dependencies, risk areas, and scalability concerns
- Support planning by estimating complexity and architectural impact
- Collaborate with Product and Project Managers on sequencing and prioritization

### Goals
- Keep the solution aligned with the intended architecture and long-term strategy
- Reduce technical debt and avoid avoidable rework
- Improve delivery predictability for complex or cross-system work

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Planning sessions and dependency mapping
- Escalations for technical risk, performance, or integration issues

### Interaction with Other Roles
- With Developers: guide implementation standards, review approach, and unblock technical decisions
- With Product Managers: explain trade-offs between roadmap scope, technical complexity, and timeline
- With Project Managers: flag dependencies, milestones, and risks that affect delivery
- With QA: align on testability, environment readiness, and operational validations

---

## DevOps / SRE

### Role Summary
DevOps and SRE roles support reliable delivery, deployment automation, and operational health. They help ensure the team can ship changes safely and recover quickly when issues arise.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines, environments, and deployment automation
- Support monitoring, logging, alerting, and incident response readiness
- Partner with Developers to improve deployment safety and rollback planning
- Validate release readiness and production health before launch
- Help document operational ownership and support expectations

### Goals
- Improve delivery speed without sacrificing stability
- Reduce deployment risk and incident impact
- Strengthen observability and operational resilience

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment check-ins
- Incident triage and postmortem reviews
- Operational readiness and monitoring discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- With Developers: support environment setup, release flow, and reliability improvements
- With Project Managers: communicate deployment windows, operational risks, and incident status
- With QA: align on smoke testing, staging readiness, and post-deploy validation
- With Product Managers: support release communication and any customer-impacting risk messaging

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations representatives bring first-hand insight from real user issues, service health, and customer feedback. They help teams prioritize improvements that reduce friction and improve the support experience.

### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring user issues, support patterns, and customer pain points
- Provide input on operational readiness, customer impact, and communication needs
- Help validate whether a release or change is safe for customer-facing support workflows
- Contribute to post-release feedback loops and issue triage
- Support handoff between product teams and support or operations teams

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and reduce escalations
- Ensure product changes are supportable and operationally safe
- Turn feedback into actionable product and process improvements

### Typical Communication
- Support escalations and incident updates
- Feedback review sessions with Product and PM teams
- Release readiness and customer communication planning

### Interaction with Other Roles
- With Product Managers: share support trends, customer pain points, and release-impact feedback
- With Project Managers: highlight schedule, communication, and operational dependencies
- With Developers: clarify failures, customer impact, and workflow constraints
- With QA: validate user scenarios and regressions that affect support or operational workflows

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles should be adapted to project size, maturity, and team structure. Not every project needs every role full-time, but each role helps clarify ownership, accountability, and collaboration.
